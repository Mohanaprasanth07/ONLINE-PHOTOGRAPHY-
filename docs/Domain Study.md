# Domain Study

## Project Title
Online Photography Print Selling System

## Domain
E-Commerce / Online Marketplace

## 1. Introduction

The Online Photography Print Selling System is an e-commerce platform designed to help photographers showcase and sell their photographs as physical prints. Customers can browse available photographs, select print options, add products to their cart, and place orders online.

The system provides a centralized platform for photographers to manage their photographs and for customers to purchase photography prints conveniently.

## 2. Domain Overview

Photography has become an important digital business area where photographers can monetize their creative work. Traditional methods of selling photo prints often depend on physical stores, exhibitions, or direct communication with customers.

An online photography print selling platform simplifies this process by providing digital photo browsing, print customization, online ordering, and order management in one system.

## 3. Existing System

In the existing system, photographers may sell their prints through:

- Physical photography shops
- Exhibitions and events
- Social media platforms
- Direct customer communication
- Third-party marketplaces

### Limitations

- Limited customer reach
- Manual order management
- Difficulty in managing multiple photographs
- Limited print customization
- Lack of centralized order tracking

## 4. Proposed System

The proposed system provides an online platform where photographers can upload and manage photographs and offer them as physical prints.

Customers can:

- Create an account
- Browse photographs
- View photo details
- Select print size and type
- Add prints to cart
- Place orders
- Track order status

Photographers can:

- Upload photographs
- Manage photo details
- Set prices
- Manage print options
- View customer orders

Administrators can:

- Manage users
- Manage photographs
- Manage orders
- Monitor the overall system

## 5. Key Features

- User Registration and Login
- Photography Gallery
- Photo Search and Browsing
- Print Size Selection
- Shopping Cart
- Order Management
- Photographer Management
- Admin Management
- Order Status Tracking

## 6. Main Entities

The major entities in the system are:

1. User
2. Photographer
3. Photo
4. Print
5. Cart
6. Order
7. Order Item
8. Payment

## 7. Target Users

### Customer
Browses photographs and purchases physical prints.

### Photographer
Uploads photographs and manages print details and prices.

### Administrator
Manages users, photographs, products, and orders.

## 8. Benefits

- Provides a wider market for photographers
- Makes photo purchasing convenient for customers
- Reduces manual order management
- Provides centralized photo and order management
- Supports online business operations

## 9. Conclusion

The Online Photography Print Selling System provides a simple and efficient e-commerce solution for selling photography prints. It connects photographers and customers through a centralized online platform while simplifying photo management, product selection, and order processing.
